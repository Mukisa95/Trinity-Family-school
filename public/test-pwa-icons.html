📋 How to See the New Icon in Your PWA

  1. Clear Browser Cache: Press Ctrl+Shift+Delete (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+Delete (Mac)
  2. Uninstall Current PWA: Go to chrome://apps/ and remove "Trinity School Online"
  3. Hard Reload: Press Ctrl+F5 (Windows) or Cmd+Shift+R (Mac)
  4. Reinstall PWA: Click the install button in the address bar
  5. Verify Icon: Check your desktop/home screen for the new icon
