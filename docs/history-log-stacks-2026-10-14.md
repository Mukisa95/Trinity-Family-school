# Compact history log and action-page links

- The existing recent-300 subscription, refresh, search, category/module/action filters and action statistics are preserved. Counts refer to individual actions, not collapsed cards.
- Filtered actions with the same pupil ID and school-local date (Africa/Nairobi, UTC+3) share one initially collapsed card. Its count and expand/collapse button reveal every original entry. New live entries preserve an open stack's state.
- Expanded cards retain the recorded label, actor, role, timestamp, changed fields, every stored metadata value and record ID. The tighter layout puts the record ID beside the actor/time when space allows. Historical pupil labels are not replaced with current names.
- Payment and banking metadata identify their pupil independently of the payment/account/loan record ID. Legacy names resolve only when unique in the existing pupil cache. Ambiguous identities and pending/invalid dates stay separate. No additional identity-resolution database reads were introduced.
- Each identifiable action card links to its saved page or a known record/workspace route. Links have prefetch disabled to avoid loading many destination pages while reading history. Cards with no reliable destination keep their full content and remain unlinked.
- New client logs save a sanitized relative page route at recording time in the optional `rp` field. Callers can supply an explicit origin with `route`. Direct writes, batch/transaction logs, audit calls and exports share this handling. Credential/redirect query parameters and API/external routes are excluded; record/period/tab context is retained. Existing history documents are not migrated or rewritten.
- New SchoolPay redistribution entries include their target pupil ID, including allocations to another family member. Payment operations and amounts are unchanged.
- Pupil/class/year/term display lookups are derived from the existing shared hooks instead of copied into state in separate effects. The existing fee-name lookup is retained.

Validation uses synthetic records: grouping at local midnight, duplicate pupil names, unknown identities/dates, filters, internal route contracts and complete log payloads across direct/atomic writers; the actual-page browser fixture checks expansion, live updates, preserved details, links, search/filters and desktop/mobile light/dark layouts. Focused TypeScript validation includes the changed sources and the application's Window declaration. QA files remain in `output/` and are excluded from publication.

Eight tests, the browser fixture and focused TypeScript check passed. The combined production build generated 109 pages and reports 416 kB first-load JavaScript for History Log and 485 kB for Pupils. The post-build check confirms PDF-renderer/photo-crop chunks remain deferred. These bundle measurements are not real-device navigation timings; the existing project-wide TypeScript issues are outside the clean focused check.
